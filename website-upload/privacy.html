Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy describes the current personal desktop application Mystery Shorts Local and its informational website. The app is operated by the owner of the Mystery Stories channel (@MysteryStories-s1w). For questions or requests, contact abosaloforai@gmail.com.

The app runs on the owner’s computer. AI generation and YouTube uploads use external services. This informational website does not provide account registration, Google sign-in, or a hosted video editor.

1. Information the app accesses and stores

  • Creative material: story ideas, prompts, drafts, titles, descriptions, generated images and narration, captions, finished videos, and editing or generation settings.
  • Connection information: the owner’s Gemini and Runway API keys, Google desktop client configuration, and Google authorization tokens. Tokens allow continuing access to the authorized YouTube account. The app does not collect the owner’s Google password.
  • YouTube information: the authorized channel’s identifier and name, checks of the intended channel handle, video identifiers returned after uploads, and information needed to resume uploads. The app does not retrieve Gmail messages, contacts, or private audience analytics.
  • Operational records: local job status, provider task identifiers, estimated spending, approval state, upload progress, and requested publication times.

2. Why YouTube permissions are requested

Mystery Shorts Local uses YouTube API Services. Its current permissions are youtube.readonly and youtube.upload. The read-only permission is used to identify and check the destination channel. The upload permission is used to send approved videos and their titles, descriptions, audience settings, synthetic-media disclosure, and publication settings to YouTube.

The app uses Google authorization and YouTube information for these connection and upload functions. It does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or send Google authorization tokens or retrieved YouTube account information to the AI generation providers. Retrieved Google user data is not used by this app to train AI models.

3. External services and sharing

  • Google Gemini: receives story instructions, an optional idea supplied by the owner, and previous locally generated story titles to help avoid repetition. Its output becomes the story draft.
  • Runway: receives image prompts, a generated character-reference image when used, narration text, and voice choices to produce images and audio. The current narration requests select an ElevenLabs model through Runway.
  • Google / YouTube: handles Google sign-in, channel identification, and video uploads. Uploaded content and its associated settings are stored by YouTube. Publication is governed by YouTube’s account restrictions and policies.
  • Support email: if you contact the operator, the email service handles your message and the information you choose to include.

Provider requests include the credentials necessary for that provider. External services process the information they receive under their applicable terms and policies, including the Google Privacy Policy, Gemini API terms, and Runway Privacy Policy. Provider retention and use can depend on the service and account plan. Avoid putting sensitive personal information in creative prompts.

4. Local storage and protection

The desktop app stores settings, credentials, authorization tokens, job records, and creative files in its local data folder. The app does not operate a separate central database or remote backup service. Credentials and tokens use files restricted to the operating-system user; the current app does not itself encrypt those files. Device access and any device backups remain under the owner’s control.

The app communicates with external providers over HTTPS. Its editing interface listens on the local computer. The public website does not contain the app’s credentials, authorization tokens, or private working files.

5. Retention and deletion

The current local version retains its settings, authorization files, and project records until the owner removes or replaces them. It does not yet perform automatic retention cleanup. The owner controls the device and can remove these files directly.

  1. Quit the local app before deleting its stored data.
  2. To remove the local connection and all app records, delete the app’s data folder, including its contents. This also removes locally saved keys, projects, generated assets, and finished videos, so first keep any creative files you want to retain.
  3. Remove any copies from your own backups if you want those copies deleted too.

Deleting local files does not delete videos already uploaded to YouTube or records retained by external providers. Uploaded videos must be managed or deleted in YouTube Studio. Contact the relevant provider about information held in its service.

6. Revoking Google access

You can revoke the app’s Google access through your Google Account permissions. Select the connection for this app and remove its access. Revocation stops future authorized API access but does not itself erase existing local files or uploaded YouTube videos. Use the deletion steps above to remove local records.

7. Visitors to this website

This static informational site has no registration forms, advertising, embedded videos, analytics scripts, or app-set tracking cookies. Its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, processes visitor information such as IP addresses for security and service operation, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

8. Changes and questions

This page will be updated when the app’s described practices change. The date above identifies this version. Send privacy questions, complaints, or requests for help with deletion to abosaloforai@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, API keys, or authorization tokens by email.
