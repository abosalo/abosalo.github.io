Mystery Shorts Local helps its owner create English mystery videos with AI-assisted writing, illustrations, narration, and captions, then review and upload them to their YouTube channel.

From idea to finished short

Create. Review. Share.

01 / CREATE

Build an original story

The desktop app uses Google Gemini for story drafts and Runway for images and narration. It assembles the finished vertical video locally on the owner’s computer.

02 / REVIEW

Make the final decision

The owner reviews the finished episode and approves it before uploading. Generation settings and spending limits are managed in the local app.

03 / SHARE

Connect the right channel

With the owner’s authorization, YouTube API Services identify the connected channel and upload approved videos. Publication settings remain subject to YouTube’s requirements and account restrictions.

A personal tool, running locally

Mystery Shorts Local is operated for the Mystery Stories channel, @MysteryStories-s1w. This website provides information about the app. The app runs on its owner’s computer and is not offered here as a public sign-up service.

Permissions & support

Know what you’re connecting.

Google sign-in takes place with Google. The app uses permission to identify the authorized YouTube channel and upload videos; it does not ask for your Google password. Read the policy for storage, external services, and how to revoke access.
